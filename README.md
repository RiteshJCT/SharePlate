# SharePlate

# Food Security
An app that connects surplus food with people who need it
# Problem:
Food from school events, restaurants, and households can go unused while people in the community experience food insecurity.
# Your app:
Users list available surplus food.
Community organizations can identify potential donations.
A matching system suggests food donation opportunities.
A dashboard tracks estimated food rescued.
# Innovation opportunity: 
Design a system that helps organizations coordinate donations based on pickup time, quantity, and food restrictions.
# What you could measure: 
How quickly users find a suitable recipient, matching accuracy, and usability feedback.
